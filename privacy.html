Idle Town Builder: Privacy Policy

By Mihira Studio · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: Idle Town Builder collects no personal information and sends nothing anywhere. Your game is saved only on your own phone.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (your town, coins, gems, upgrades and settings) in the app's own storage on your device. This save never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and nobody else can either. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising (the "watch a video" rewards are given without showing an ad), no analytics and no crash reporting. It makes no network requests while you play. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Permissions

Vibration makes the phone buzz softly when you chop a tree or mine a rock. You can turn it off in the game's settings.
Internet is included by the app framework the game is built with. The game does not use it.

Purchases

Gems, coins and the town pass are earned by playing. The game sells nothing for real money; prices shown in the shop are marked "coming soon" and cannot be bought. If that ever changes, purchases would be handled by Google Play, and this policy would be updated first.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
